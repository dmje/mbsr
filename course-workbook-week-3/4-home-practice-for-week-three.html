Home practice for Week Three

Mindful movement

For week 3 we’re introducing a new practice which we’re going to do this week instead of the body scan. Remember though that the body scan will always be available to you – just add it to your armoury of meditations that will grow over time as you become more familiar with the practice. You can obviously come back to it anytime you like!

This week we have another 30 minute practice: mindful movement.

As with the body scan, mindful movement is here to encourage us to “come home to the body”. Tuning in to your body, to your breathing, to your movement – helps you become more attuned to the messages it is sending out. You’re getting used to spending time “in” your body – this sounds strange but as we said earlier, we’re so used to thinking “from our heads” all the time that many of us forget that our bodies are even here!

The movements we are going to do are all non-strenuous but of course the rule applies here as it does to absolutely everything on this course: if you experience particular discomfort with a movement, stop and assess if this is right for you. Mindfulness practice is about listening to and understanding our bodies and minds, each of which is unique. So if something doesn't feel right, can you use this as an opportunity to bring kindness to yourself rather than self-judgement? This isn’t power yoga, or trying to come first in a race – there is nothing competitive about any of this work – it should be supportive, generous, gentle and compassionate.

Unpleasant Events Journal

Last week we had the pleasant events log. This week – you guessed it – it’s unpleasant events.

So as per week 2, this week as well as the mindful movement, please print out and fill in the following log. See if you can do at least one per day.

As with the pleasant events, don’t go big – no huge traumas, just little irritating or annoying things that you bump into during the week.

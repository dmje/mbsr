# Week 5, Day 1

## Formal practice today

The formal practice shifts this week: [[Mindfulness of breathing]], 30 minutes if you can, or steady at a shorter duration and build. Sit with the breath the way you sat with the body.

## Informal practice today

Start Taking in the Good today: notice one good thing as it happens, stay with it for 15-20 seconds, and let it land. Deliberately letting the good register is a practice, not a mood.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindfulness of breathing plus one deliberate Taking in the Good moment. Small and done beats large and skipped.

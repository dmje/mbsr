# Week 5, Day 5

## Formal practice today

[[Mindfulness of breathing]]. Try including ten minutes of awareness of thoughts in today's sit: watching thoughts arrive and pass, like weather.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once today, plus STOP once if the day holds any stress.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindfulness of breathing plus one deliberate Taking in the Good moment. Small and done beats large and skipped.

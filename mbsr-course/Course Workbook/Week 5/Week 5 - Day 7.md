# Week 5, Day 7

## Formal practice today

Lighter practice today if you want one, or the full sit. Half the course is behind you; that is worth registering.

## Informal practice today

Look back over the week's Taking in the Good moments. What does your attention find now that it has been asked to look for the good?

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindfulness of breathing plus one deliberate Taking in the Good moment. Small and done beats large and skipped.

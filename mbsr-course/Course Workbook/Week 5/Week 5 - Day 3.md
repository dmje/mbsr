# Week 5, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Mindfulness of breathing]]. When the mind is very busy, widen to the whole body breathing rather than the small sensations at the nostrils.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once today. Also try it on something very small: warm water, a clean plate, a moment of quiet.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindfulness of breathing plus one deliberate Taking in the Good moment. Small and done beats large and skipped.

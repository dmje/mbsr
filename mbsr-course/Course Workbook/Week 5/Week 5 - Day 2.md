# Week 5, Day 2

## Formal practice today

[[Mindfulness of breathing]] again. Attention will slide off the breath constantly; that is what breaths of awareness look like. Return each time.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once today, plus spend two minutes this evening writing three things that went right, however small.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindfulness of breathing plus one deliberate Taking in the Good moment. Small and done beats large and skipped.

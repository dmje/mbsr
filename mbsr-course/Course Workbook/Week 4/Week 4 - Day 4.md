# Week 4, Day 4

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] or body scan. Notice the difference between a real stressor and the body's alarm about a stressor; most of what we call stress is the alarm.

## Informal practice today

STOP once today. If today contains an actual stressful episode, see if the STOP can happen in the middle of it rather than only before or after.

## If the day goes sideways

A three-minute breathing space, done deliberately, plus a quick STOP at one transition today. Ten honest minutes beats an abandoned forty.

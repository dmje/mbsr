# Week 4, Day 7

## Formal practice today

Lighter practice today, your choice from the week. Week four is often where people feel the course land; be gentle with yourself this weekend.

## Informal practice today

Look back over the week's thermometer readings. Where did the numbers spike? What in your day reliably moves the number up and what reliably moves it down?

## If the day goes sideways

A three-minute breathing space, done deliberately, plus a quick STOP at one transition today. Ten honest minutes beats an abandoned forty.

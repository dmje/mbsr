# Week 4, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]]. After your practice, take a second Stress Thermometer reading; for most people ten minutes of body-centred attention moves the number, and knowing that is worth more than the movement itself.

## Informal practice today

STOP once today, plus take a thermometer reading at one difficult moment of the day.

## If the day goes sideways

A three-minute breathing space, done deliberately, plus a quick STOP at one transition today. Ten honest minutes beats an abandoned forty.

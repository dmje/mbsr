## Practice STOP this week

Keep practicing STOP as much as you can this week. You might try putting little post it notes around the house to help you remember: on the fridge, above your computer, on the mirror in the bathroom. Wherever you need to be reminded to take a moment, pause, take a breath and check in with your experience. 

Aside from the physiological benefits of this practice, the more we can punctuate our day with moments of rest and awareness, the more natural and effortless it will become for our mind and body to enter these quieter, more restorative, restful states. Just like any practice, the more you do it, the easier and more natural it will feel. 

It's ok to use STOP as a remedial measure when we feel activated, stressed-out and times are tough. But using it as a preventative measure as well can help prevent stress from overwhelming you.

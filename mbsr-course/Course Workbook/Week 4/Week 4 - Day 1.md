# Week 4, Day 1

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] remains the core formal practice this week. Before you begin, locate today's reading on the Stress Thermometer out of ten; write it beside your practice log when you finish.

## Informal practice today

Place your STOP reminders where you will meet them: a post-it on the monitor, a sticker on the kettle. Actually stop once today.

## If the day goes sideways

A three-minute breathing space, done deliberately, plus a quick STOP at one transition today. Ten honest minutes beats an abandoned forty.

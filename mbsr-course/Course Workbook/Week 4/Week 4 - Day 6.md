# Week 4, Day 6

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] or body scan. Today, watch for the moment in the practice when the body first settles, and notice what that settling actually feels like.

## Informal practice today

STOP once today, plus a moment of full attention in one conversation.

## If the day goes sideways

A three-minute breathing space, done deliberately, plus a quick STOP at one transition today. Ten honest minutes beats an abandoned forty.

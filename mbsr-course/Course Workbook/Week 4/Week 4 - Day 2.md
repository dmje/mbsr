# Week 4, Day 2

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] or the body scan. While practising, notice any weather in the mind about the week's stress; the point is only to see it clearly.

## Informal practice today

Do the STOP practice at least once today. When you stop, look for the stress reaction in the body before you carry on.

## If the day goes sideways

A three-minute breathing space, done deliberately, plus a quick STOP at one transition today. Ten honest minutes beats an abandoned forty.

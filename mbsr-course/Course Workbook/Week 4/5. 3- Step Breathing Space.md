# 3-Step Breathing Space

Here is a shorter meditation practice, sometimes known as GAP or the 3-step breathing space (3-Step).

Short practices like the 3-Step are extremely good for: 

1. Breaking us out of autopilot and disrupting the narrative playing in our heads. 
2. Opening us up to how we are actually feeling.
3. Calming the nervous system.
4. Giving us the space to consider what we might need to best support ourselves. 
5. Boosting creativity. 

The 3-Step Breathing Space typically lasts around 10 minutes and is a really great compliment to a longer practice. Perhaps if you practice in the morning then you could fit this in the afternoon or evening. Or if you practice in the evening then squeezing this short practice into your morning or lunch break could really help you calm the nervous system and bring more awareness and kindness to yourself. 

Remember, the more mindful moments and practices you can punctuate your day with, the more effortless your mindfulness will become and the more you will feel the benefits of practice. Each reinforces the other. 

###  How to Practice the 3-Step

Step 1 - Eyes open or closed acknowledge all experiences in this moment, those in the body, heart and mind. Simply noticing what is here in your experience right now.

Step 2 - Narrow your awareness on the movement of the breath in the centre of the body, at the abdomen.

Step 3 - Now broaden your awareness once again to include everything in your experience, holding it all with kindness, allowing it to be there and gently bringing an acceptance to it all.

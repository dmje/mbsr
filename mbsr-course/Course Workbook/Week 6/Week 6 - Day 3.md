# Week 6, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Mindfulness of breathing]]. If feelings surface during the sit, find them in the body and stay with the sensations rather than the story.

## Informal practice today

Mindful listening once today, plus Taking in the Good once: perhaps the good moment someone offers you that you nearly missed.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful breathing and one brief mindful listening moment. If nothing else, three breaths before you speak today.

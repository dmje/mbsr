# Week 6, Day 2

## Formal practice today

[[Mindfulness of breathing]], with awareness of thoughts as part of the sit. Expect it to feel strange at first; noticing the strange feeling is also the practice.

## Informal practice today

Mindful listening once today. Leaving a small silence before replying counts as a full practice of it.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful breathing and one brief mindful listening moment. If nothing else, three breaths before you speak today.

# Home practice for Week Six

This week, your formal practice turns outwards as well as inwards: the people around you become part of the field of practice.

## 1. Formal practice

Continue with your mindful breathing practice for at least 30 minutes, at least five days out of seven. As part of this week's sitting practice, include some time practising awareness of thoughts and emotions, if you have not already made room for it.

## 2. Mindful listening, once a day

Once a day, have a conversation where you practise listening with full awareness. Pick an ordinary conversation rather than a high-stakes one to begin with. Your job is not to be a perfect listener, but to notice what actually happens in you while someone else is speaking:

- Can you stay with what they are saying, or does your mind race ahead to your reply?
- What happens in your body? Where does the tension of wanting to speak live?
- Can you leave a small silence before you respond, rather than jumping in?

If it helps you reflect, note the conversation in the table below. Do not use real names if you would rather not write them down; "my brother", "a colleague" is enough.

## 3. Keep practising Taking in the Good

Continue with Taking in the Good this week. Noticing the good in your relationships, the small kindnesses done and received, is a natural pairing with this week's theme.

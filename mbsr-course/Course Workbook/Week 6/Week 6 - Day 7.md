# Week 6, Day 7

## Formal practice today

Lighter practice today if you need it. Otherwise, a longer sit than usual, since the retreat is designed to build on your current capacity.

## Informal practice today

Read back over the week's listening log. Where does your attention go when someone else is speaking: to them, to your reply, to your phone? No judgement; just noticing, with a bit of practice behind it.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful breathing and one brief mindful listening moment. If nothing else, three breaths before you speak today.

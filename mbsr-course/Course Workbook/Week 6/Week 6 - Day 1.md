# Week 6, Day 1

## Formal practice today

[[Mindfulness of breathing]] for 30 minutes, including time on awareness of thoughts and emotions. Notice any resistance to watching your own mind; it is just more weather.

## Informal practice today

Mindful listening once today: one conversation where your job is to notice what happens in you while the other person speaks. Log it.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful breathing and one brief mindful listening moment. If nothing else, three breaths before you speak today.

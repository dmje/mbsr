# Week 8, Day 6

## Formal practice today

Unguided practice, of any kind, for whatever time is honest today. Notice, if you can, the difference between practising to complete the course and practising because it is what you do now.

## Informal practice today

Finish your practice plan today if you have not: duration, time, place, one informal practice, one diary note a month from now.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of any practice you have learned this course, plus one Taking in the Good moment. That is a complete day's practice this late in the course.

# Week 8, Day 1

## Formal practice today

Practise today without guidance if you can: breathing, body, or the practice of your choice. The course is nearly over; your practice is nearly yours.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once, plus one deliberate informal practice: one activity, full attention.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of any practice you have learned this course, plus one Taking in the Good moment. That is a complete day's practice this late in the course.

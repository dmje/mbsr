# Week 8, Day 7

## Formal practice today

A final practice of the course, your own choice. Take a moment at the end to acknowledge eight weeks of practice; it is a genuinely substantial thing to have done.

## Informal practice today

Put the practice plan somewhere you will see it, and set the diary reminder for your one-month review. The eighth week is the rest of your life.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of any practice you have learned this course, plus one Taking in the Good moment. That is a complete day's practice this late in the course.

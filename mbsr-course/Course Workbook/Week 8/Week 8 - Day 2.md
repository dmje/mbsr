# Week 8, Day 2

## Formal practice today

Unguided sit, your choice of practice. Notice what you do when nobody is telling you what to notice; that is the emerging shape of your own practice.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once, plus a three-minute breathing space at one point in the day.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of any practice you have learned this course, plus one Taking in the Good moment. That is a complete day's practice this late in the course.

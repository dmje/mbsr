# Week 8, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Loving Kindness]] today, guided or unguided. The practice of inclining the heart is worth carrying beyond the course; today is a reminder of how it goes.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once. If it suits you, offer one small kindness today deliberately, and notice how it lands in you rather than in them.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of any practice you have learned this course, plus one Taking in the Good moment. That is a complete day's practice this late in the course.

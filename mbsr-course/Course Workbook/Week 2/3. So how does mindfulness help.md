## So…how does mindfulness help?

You may experience the benefits of mindfulness practice in a broad variety of ways over the duration of the course, some more measurable and tangible than others. As we will see next week, there are a range of psychological traits and attitudes that we develop through mindfulness practice that can transform our inner life, and we will be reflecting on this process as we go.

We’ll also spend much of the rest of the course discovering specifically how mindfulness helps us respond to stress. 

Here’s a quick table to outline some of these benefits:


| Living a Stressful Life Can lead to:              | Integrating Mindfulness can lead to: |
|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| An inability to focus or hold concentration       | The ability to pay attention and flexibly move attention                        |
| An inability to really listen to others                    | Becoming a better more attentive listener            |
| Unable to manage or cope in difficult situations  | Greater resilience                                      |
| High heart rate                                   | Lower and improved heart rate                        |
| High Blood pressure                               | Lower and improved blood pressure                    |
| Poor sleep                                        | Better sleep & better quality of sleep               |
| Low self-esteem                                   | Growing self-assuredness                                   |
| Consumed by negative self-talk / limiting beliefs | Compassion and kindness towards oneself                        |

# We need to talk about stress

You probably noticed that the S in MBSR stands for “stress” – in fact it may be this S that attracted you to the MBSR course in the first place…

So – let’s start by asking this question: what causes stress for you?

Make a list of the things you find most stressful:

Here's a list of some "common stressful things":

- Finances
- Job
- Children
- Being pulled in different directions
- The news
- Lack of sleep
- Conflict in relationships
- Family
- Incompetence of co-workers
- Uncertainty

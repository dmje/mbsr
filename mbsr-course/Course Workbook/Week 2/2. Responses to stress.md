# Responses to stress

Many studies have been done about the causes and effects of stress – and you may well have seen some of these – but one of the most well researched and recognised was done by Palmer & Dryden in 1995 Palmer, S. and Dryden, W. (1995). [Counselling for Stress Problems](https://www.perlego.com/book/861143/counselling-for-stress-problems-pdf). London: Sage.

This identified a huge range of stress responses, from biological ones such as high blood pressure, to behavioural – drug and alcohol abuse, loss of appetite, insomnia – to emotional effects such as depression, anger, guilt and anxiety.

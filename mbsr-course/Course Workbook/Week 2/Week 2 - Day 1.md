# Week 2, Day 1

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]] continues as the backbone of the week, 30 minutes or whatever you can sustain. Notice any change in how the body registers since week one.

## Informal practice today

Start the Pleasant Events Journal today, and add one entry before you sleep: one thing during the day that was pleasant, however small, and what you noticed in body and mind at the time.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Then one pleasant event entry before bed, even a single line.

# Week 2, Day 5

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]]. Notice before you begin what resistance or eagerness is present, and let the scan start from there.

## Informal practice today

One pleasant event entry. Try to catch one moment today when you are waiting, and simply stand in it rather than filling it.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Then one pleasant event entry before bed, even a single line.

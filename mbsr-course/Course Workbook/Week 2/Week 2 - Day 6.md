# Week 2, Day 6

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]]. Notice whether the practice is easier to begin on some days than others, and whether the pattern has anything to do with what the day holds.

## Informal practice today

One pleasant event entry, plus a moment of full attention during one conversation today.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Then one pleasant event entry before bed, even a single line.

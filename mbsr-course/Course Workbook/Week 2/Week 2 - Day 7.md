# Week 2, Day 7

## Formal practice today

A lighter or shorter sit if you need it, or the full scan if you are enjoying it. Continuity is what matters today.

## Informal practice today

Look back over the Pleasant Events Journal; read your six entries in one sitting. Notice what kind of things your attention found when it was sent looking.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Then one pleasant event entry before bed, even a single line.

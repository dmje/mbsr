# Week 2, Day 4

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]]. Attention wanders and comes back; that is the movement of the practice, not a sign of failure. Today, gently celebrate the comebacks.

## Informal practice today

One pleasant event entry, plus three breaths at one transition point today: before opening the laptop, before starting the car, before walking through a door.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Then one pleasant event entry before bed, even a single line.

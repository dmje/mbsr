# Week 2, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]]. While scanning, pay particular attention to any difference between the left and right sides of the body; comparison is a way of sharpening attention.

## Informal practice today

One pleasant event entry. Try one routine activity today done deliberately slowly, at 80 per cent of your usual pace.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Then one pleasant event entry before bed, even a single line.

# Week 3, Day 6

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] or body scan. In whichever you choose, spend part of the time deliberately noticing what is pleasant or neutral in the body, not just what aches.

## Informal practice today

One unpleasant event entry, plus one pleasant event entry: this week both journals are worth keeping; most days hold both kinds of weather.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

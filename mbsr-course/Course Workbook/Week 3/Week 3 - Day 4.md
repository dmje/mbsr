# Week 3, Day 4

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] or body scan, your choice. Try the STOP practice once today at a moment of mild stress: Stop, Take a breath, Observe, Proceed.

## Informal practice today

One unpleasant event entry, plus three breaths at one transition moment in the day.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

# Week 3, Day 1

## Formal practice today

This week [[Mindful Movement]] alternates with the body scan. Today, do the guided mindful movement practice. Move within your limits; awareness is the practice, not the stretch.

## Informal practice today

Start the Unpleasant Events Journal today: one entry for something mildly unpleasant, and what you noticed in body and mind at the time. Mild is the right level.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

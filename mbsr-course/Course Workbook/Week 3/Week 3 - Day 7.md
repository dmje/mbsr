# Week 3, Day 7

## Formal practice today

A lighter practice today, or your choice from the week's practices. Congratulations: three weeks is roughly where the practice begins to feel like something you do rather than something you try.

## Informal practice today

Read back through the week's unpleasant event entries. Notice whether your reactions had shapes you recognise; the shape is where awareness gets its purchase.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

# Week 3, Day 2

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]] again, or the body scan if your body is tired or unwell. Both are full practices; neither is the warm-up for the other.

## Informal practice today

One unpleasant event entry. Also do one routine activity today with full attention at a slow pace.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

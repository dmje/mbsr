# Week 3, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]]. Notice the sensations of the movement from the inside: stretching, releasing, the wobble of balance, the breath moving with you.

## Informal practice today

One unpleasant event entry. When it is written, read it back and ask: where was this in the body?

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

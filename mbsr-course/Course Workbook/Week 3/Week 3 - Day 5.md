# Week 3, Day 5

## Formal practice today

[[Mindful Movement]]. If you skipped a day this week, begin again without commentary; beginning again is the core skill of the whole course.

## Informal practice today

One unpleasant event entry. Try noticing, once today, the body's immediate response to an interruption: a phone ringing, a demand, a knock.

## If the day goes sideways

Ten minutes of mindful movement, or a shortened body scan. Then one line in the unpleasant events journal before sleep.

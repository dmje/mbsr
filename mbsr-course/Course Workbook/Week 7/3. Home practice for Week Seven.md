# Home practice for Week Seven

This is the week the course begins handing the practice back to you. From here on, the emphasis shifts from following guidance to finding your own way of practising, which is exactly what you will need when the course ends.

## Formal practice, your choice

Practise for at least 30 minutes, at least five days out of seven, choosing from:

1. **Loving kindness.** The practice introduced this week. Especially valuable if the week has felt reactive or prickly.
2. **The mountain practice.** Also introduced this week. Especially valuable if the week has felt stormy.
3. **Mindful breathing**, now widened to include stretches of awareness of thoughts and emotions.

## One day without a recording

Choose one practice day this week and do your sitting practice without any guidance at all. This is the first step toward practising independently. Expect it to feel different, and a bit like the early weeks of the course: quieter, slower, and less certain. All of that is fine.

## Keep Taking in the Good

Continue with Taking in the Good as often as you can, and notice how quickly it has become part of how you look at a day.

## The coming retreat

Between this week and next sits the half-day retreat: an extended silent practice session, using everything you have learned so far. Have a look at the retreat page now, so you know what to expect, and can arrange the time for it.

# Week 7, Day 5

## Formal practice today

Your choice, guided or unguided. Whichever you choose, notice whether you are choosing from what you need or what you think you should do.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once today, plus a mindful walk, even ten minutes: attention in the feet, the sounds, the air.

## If the day goes sideways

Fifteen minutes, guided or not, of whichever practice today most needs. Days like this are not lost practice; they are the practice.

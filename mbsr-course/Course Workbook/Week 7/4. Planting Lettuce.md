# Planting Lettuce

Here is a passage from the late, great teacher Thich Nhat Hanh, in his book *Peace is Every Step (1990)*:

>When you plant lettuce, if it does not grow well, you don’t blame the lettuce. You look for reasons it is not doing well. It may need fertiliser, or more water, or less sun. You never blame the lettuce. Yet if we have problems with our friends or family, we blame the other person. But, like the lettuce, if we know how to take care of them, they will grow well. Blaming has no positive effect at all, nor does trying to persuade using reason and argument. That is my experience. No blame, no reasoning, no argument, just understanding. If you understand, and you show that you understand, you can love, and the situation will change.”

# Week 7, Day 4

## Formal practice today

Today is the unguided day: sit for your usual time with no recording at all. Slower, quieter, less certain; all of that is what independent practice actually feels like.

## Informal practice today

Taking in the Good once today. Notice whether practising unguided felt like loss or like freedom; both answers are common, and both are fine.

## If the day goes sideways

Fifteen minutes, guided or not, of whichever practice today most needs. Days like this are not lost practice; they are the practice.

# How will this course help me?

The MBSR course is evidence based, with four decades of research examining the direct, positive effects it has on attendees' health and well-being.

Here are some of the benefits from attending the 8-week MBSR course:

- Reduce pain, stress and anxiety, as well as learn ways to cope better when they arise.
- Increase attentional focus and cognitive performance.
- Reduce the mind's reactivity, both emotional and cognitive.
- Reduce heart rate, blood pressure, and reduce inflammation.
- Increase kindness and compassion towards oneself and others.
- Increase equanimity (the special sauce of meditation practice): the ability to stay balanced and centred through life's ups and downs.

The MBSR course is not – and should never be used as – a substitute for medical intervention. However, as we will discover later on in the course, a consistent mindfulness practice can have a number of health benefits both physical and mental that can help with a whole range of ongoing ailments.

Although mindful practices have their roots in different spiritual traditions, it should be noted that the MBSR course is secular in nature. Mindfulness practice is a way of exploring and opening to what it means to be human, there is nothing that you must believe to get the most out of the course, we simply ask that you show up and practice with us.

# Week 1, Day 5

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]] again. If sleepiness arrives during the scan, notice it; it is a body event like any other, and one you can be aware of.

## Informal practice today

Your routine activity. Try using three breaths at a red light, or while the kettle boils, or while waiting for something to load.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Even ten honest minutes keeps the thread unbroken.

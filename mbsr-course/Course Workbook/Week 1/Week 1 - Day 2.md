# Week 1, Day 2

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]] again today. If yesterday's sit was hard to settle into, expect that; settling is a skill, and it is built by repetition.

## Informal practice today

Repeat your chosen routine activity with full attention. Notice whether it is any easier the second time.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Even ten honest minutes keeps the thread unbroken.

# Week 1, Day 3

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]]. If you have not already done the raisin exercise, eat one snack mindfully today: one thing, attended to, as if you had never eaten before.

## Informal practice today

Your routine activity, done with awareness. Also try putting your full attention into the first three steps of any walk you take today.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Even ten honest minutes keeps the thread unbroken.

# Week 1, Day 6

## Formal practice today

[[Body Scan]]. Six days in. If you have skipped a day, that is information, not failure; today is a new sit.

## Informal practice today

Your routine activity plus a mindful conversation today: notice while listening whether you are already rehearsing your reply.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Even ten honest minutes keeps the thread unbroken.

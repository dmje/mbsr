# Week 1, Day 1

## Formal practice today

Today's formal practice is the [[Body Scan]], the full 30-minute recording if you can manage it. If 30 minutes is not possible today, do 15. Showing up counts more than duration.

## Informal practice today

Choose one routine activity, brushing your teeth, making a cup of tea, and do it with full attention today. Nothing else added; just one thing, done properly.

## If the day goes sideways

Do a three-minute breathing space: one minute noticing what is here, one minute with the breath, one minute widening to the body as a whole. It is a small thing, and it counts as practice.

# Week 1, Day 7

## Formal practice today

A lighter sit today if you want one: any practice from this week, for whatever time you have. The aim of a light day is to keep the thread unbroken rather than to achieve anything.

## Informal practice today

Look back over the week: note in your log one thing you noticed this week that you would not have noticed a month ago.

## If the day goes sideways

Sit for ten minutes with a shortened body scan: feet, legs, back and shoulders, head and face. Even ten honest minutes keeps the thread unbroken.

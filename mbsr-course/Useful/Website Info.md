Our vision at Moment to Moment is to bring mindfulness, meditation and the philosophy, art and science surrounding it to as many people as we can. Moment to Moment is a space to teach secular meditation and mindfulness, but without shying away from the deeper, more esoteric stuff. We share a hard-nosed scientific outlook, and tremendous passion and enthusiasm for mindfulness’s therapeutic potential in tackling stress, anxiety, depression and many other mental health difficulties, of which there is a large scientific evidence-base. Whilst mindfulness is a powerful therapeutic practice, it is also a tool for radical self-discovery that can transform our outlook on life, our relationships with our selves and other people. Mindfulness meditation is a path to wisdom and insight that has roots in ancient spiritual traditions. It takes us on a journey through different cultures, both ancient and modern, through art, through philosophy and now through science in the modern age. It’s one hell of an adventure and with our 30+ combined years of meditation practice, our broad knowledge of the science of meditation and different spiritual traditions we want to support people to go as deep as they like.

We believe that mindfulness meditation practice is ultimately what you make it. You might have a 10-minute daily practice you use to feel a bit calmer, or a 2-hour daily practice going deep into the nature of awareness! We have something for everyone. So stay tuned.

8 week Mindfulness-Based Stress-Reduction (MBSR) Course

The Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) programme is the most well-established and well-researched mindfulness course, taught all over the world, and scientifically proven to help you reduce stress and cope better with depression and anxiety. 

Over the last 30 years scientific research into mindfulness has flourished, typically through studying the 8-week MBSR course. Neuroscience, psychology and physical medicine have all documented an astoundingly diverse set of benefits from learning to meditate.

We recommend the 8-week Mindfulness-Based Stress-Reduction course as the best way to learn the skill of meditation. We introduce you to the principles of mindfulness practice, the physiology of stress, the science of meditation, teach you a range of meditation practices, give you the tools and skills to manage stress, anxiety and pain, and help you solidify this transformative skill into a habit that you can integrate further into your life and reap the greatest benefits from.

The online course consists of eight, 2-hour sessions and a half-day retreat. Each session involves guided meditations, group discussion and dialogue, teachings on a range of topics such as mindfulness, pain, stress, relationships, as well as person-specific guidance on developing your home meditation practice. 

Your commitments:

- 30 minutes formal meditation practice for 5 out of 7 days each week, for 8 weeks.

- Journaling for both formal and informal mindfulness practices.

- Attendance of all 8 online sessions.

- Optional half-day retreat

# Informal practice log

Formal practice builds the muscle; informal practice is where you actually use it. Informal practice means bringing full awareness to an ordinary activity while you are doing it: drinking tea, walking to the car, washing up, listening to someone.

## How to use it

Once a day, ideally at the end of the day, take two minutes and write down one informal practice you remember. If you cannot remember one, that is worth noting too: it tells you something about where the day went.

You might also note any STOP practice or breathing spaces you used in a difficult moment, and what happened when you did.

## Ideas when you cannot think of one

- One cup of tea or coffee, drunk with full attention, phone elsewhere.
- The first thirty seconds of your shower.
- Brushing your teeth.
- Walking anywhere, noticing the feeling of the ground.
- Stopping at a closed door before opening it, and taking one breath.
- Listening to someone without interrupting.
- Eating the first three bites of a meal with full attention.
- One STOP practice: Stop, Take a breath, Observe, Proceed.

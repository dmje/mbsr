# Mindful movement in your practice

Mindful movement carries exactly the same awareness training as the body scan and the sitting practice; the object of attention is simply a body that is moving. In the standard MBSR curriculum it sits alongside the body scan from around week three onwards, and it deserves a proper place in your practice too, especially on days when sitting and lying still feel impossible.

The starting point is the [[Mindful Movement]] transcript. This page is about fitting movement into the flow of your week.

## The principles, briefly

- It is not exercise, and it is not about achieving a shape. Any posture has three valid versions on any day: doing it, modifying it, or resting and imagining doing it.
- You go to your edge and not beyond it. Pain is information, never an achievement.
- The attention is on sensation: the stretch, the tremble, the weight shifting, the breath responding. If the attention is on doing it right, that is thinking, not practising.
- Slow is the whole point. If you can do it comfortably at that pace you are moving at the right speed for practice.

## When to use it

- **From week three**, alternate movement with the body scan across your practice days rather than treating movement as an optional extra. A common pattern is movement one day, body scan the next, sitting practice each day.
- **On restless days.** When sitting practice keeps collapsing into planning, movement gives the attention something physical to hold, and it often settles the mind enough to sit afterwards.
- **Before sitting.** Ten minutes of slow movement before a sit can make a session noticeably steadier.
- **When you have no time.** Five slow movements done properly are a real practice, not a compromise version of one.

## A note on safety

Work within your limits, and take seriously anything that is more than mild discomfort. If you manage any kind of physical condition, treat the sequences as you would any new exercise and check with your GP or physio if you are unsure that movement practice is what your body needs right now.

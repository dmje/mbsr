## The raisin practice

This may sound weird, but take a moment to go and find a small plate and a few raisins.

- When you have these, place the raisins on the plate. Look at them. Now choose a raisin.
- Place the raisin in the palm of your left hand. Look at it. What does it look like? Notice the shape, size, weight. Notice how the light plays off the texture on its surface.
- Turn it over using your other hand. What does this feel like?
- Pick the raisin up. Smell it. Squish it a tiny bit and smell it again.
- Hold it up to your ear. Listen to your raisin! Squash it a tiny bit again, and listen some more. What do you hear?
- Gently place the raisin on your lips. Feel it against your lips. Allow it gently into your mouth. Don't chew yet, just allow it to sit on your tongue. What do you notice?
- Take one bite. What do you notice now?
- Now chew it. Continue to notice exactly what this feels like.
- What does your tongue and mouth do after you have swallowed?
- What does it feel like to no longer have the raisin? What remains?

You can do this again with another raisin if you like and see if you notice anything else or different the second time around…

You may have noticed that raisins are actually pretty amazing. That thing that we all stuff into our mouths in their hundreds in cakes and chocolate bars can be looked at in a totally different way, provided you pay really close attention to it.

This, in a nutshell, is mindfulness.

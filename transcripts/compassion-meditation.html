Compassion Practice - (Loving Kindness)

Options:

'May you be happy' - Option 1

Keep it simple and just stick to the phrases,

May you be happy May you be safe May you be free from suffering May you accept yourself as you are

Embodied Compassion - Option 2

Extend a felt sense of positive regard out to others, both connecting with another person as well as your own feelings of loving kindness towards them.

Script

Start by imagining a loved one, perhaps a dear friend, family member, a pet, don't overthink it, just take a moment to settle on one person that you have relatively uncomplicated feelings of love and affection towards. Once you have a clear mental image of them in your mind, take a moment just to notice how you feel as you picture this person in your mind's eye. Gather a sense perhaps of what they mean to you, how you feel towards them. Try to connect with them in the fullest, truest sense that you can. Seeing their basic desire to be happy, to be loved, but also knowing that they suffer, that they are fallible, imperfect and prone to failure.

Take a few moments to really connect with this being.

Can you feel into your own deepest intentions for this individual's happiness. Opening to these intentions of care, Sensing into your intentions to see them happy, peaceful and free from suffering.

as you do this notice how you feel in the body. Can you feel any sensations of warmth in the body, a sense of opening and expansion perhaps, a tenderness, a softening, an energy in the body? Where do you feel these sensations in the body? In the chest, the heart area, in the throat, in the abdomen?

If there is any resistance in the body or the mind, notice this also.

Noticing whatever arises in the body as you connect with your intentions to see this person be happy, to be free from suffering.

And if the mind wanders, gently bringing it back to the image of this loved one and your immediate sensations in the body, reconnecting with your experience in this moment.

If it feels more natural for you, you might try conveying these intentions in simple phrases, gently dropping them into your experience, seeing how they land with you, like watching the ripples in a pond. Phrases like 'may you be happy', 'may you be safe', 'may you accept yourself as you are', 'may you live with ease'. Just something that reflects your own authentic wish for this person's happiness, really connecting with the intention behind these words and how you feel in the body as each phrase lands.

Don't force yourself or try to engineer any particular feelings, this practice is simply about staying open, connecting with the intentions of kindness and compassion and allowing yourself to feel whatever comes up.

Can you imagine radiating out any feelings in the body towards this loved one, nourishing them in these feelings of kindness and well-wishing.

Now letting go of this image of a loved one as you stay connected to this embodied sense of kindness and compassion. connecting with these feelings in the body. And instead of a loved one, picturing yourself now in your minds eye. Just like you did with a loved one, can you really get a sense of yourself, as you are in your fullness, connecting with your own fundamental desire for happiness, as well as the wounds of being human that you hold on to, of suffering, imperfection, and failure. Picturing yourself smiling back at you. Can you see yourself as also deserving of love and kindness, extending the same feelings of unconditional care and kindness to yourself, just as you did a loved one. Feeling into these intentions of kindness for yourself, and noticing how this feels in the body as you offer yourself those same feelings. Imagining yourself being warmed by them, nourishing you, like sunlight at the window.

If it helps, offer yourself the same phrases as before, 'May I be happy', 'May I be safe', 'May I accept myself as I am', 'May I live with ease', or you can gently place a hand on your heart, feeling into any warmth and tenderness there, allowing yourself to feel whatever arises as you wish yourself well, connecting with your own intentions of kindness and self-care.

If the mind becomes distracted, just bring your awareness back to the image of yourself and the sensations in your body in this moment, wishing yourself well, wishing yourself happiness.

Letting go of this image of yourself now. and bringing an image of a neutral person to mind. Someone you don't feel any particular way about, but who you can clearly picture. Maybe a shop clerk, a colleague you don't know very well, or someone you see on your commute to work. Beginning to get a real sense of this person, you might know little about them, but they too share a basic human wish for happiness and well-being. Feeling into this common humanity, seeing them as also in need of the same love and kindness that we all need. Staying connected with the body as you do this, imagining those same feelings of warmth, love and kindness radiating out, offering them wholeheartedly to the image of this more neutral person.

'May you be happy', 'may you be safe', 'May you accept yourself as you are', 'May you live with ease.'

Letting go of the image of a neutral person now and bringing to mind someone you find to be more challenging, perhaps someone you have strained or difficult feelings towards. Nothing too intense here, aiming around a 4 or 5 out of 10 in terms of the intensity of feeling. It may be a friend with whom you are experiencing difficulty at the moment, a colleague you don't get on with, or perhaps someone in the media or in the public eye with whom you have more difficult feelings towards.

Once you've settled on an image of this individual, as before, really take a moment to get a sense of this person before you. Beginning to connect to their basic human wish to be happy, their wish to not suffer, and their shared need to be loved. Beginning to open yourself to the possibility of wanting this person too to be well and be happy, connecting with any intentions of kindness, wishing this person well.

Staying connected to the body as you do this, can you allow those same feelings of compassion to radiate out to this more challenging person.

Offering phrases if that helps you connect with these intentions of well-wishing. "May you be happy," 'may you be safe', 'may you accept yourself as you are', 'may you live with ease'.

Now dropping this image of a more challenging person and directing this embodied sense of kindness and compassion to all living beings. You might picture these intentions of kindness and feelings in the body as a tremendous source of light, radiating out more and more to include all the living beings on this earth.

'May all beings be happy' 'may all beings be safe', 'may all beings accept themselves as they are', 'may all beings live with ease'.

Allowing all beings to be filled with this light, and be liberated from suffering.

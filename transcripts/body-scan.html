Welcome to the body scan.

This practice will last around 30 minutes

Typically the practice is done lying down but you can sit in a chair if that is more comfortable.

If you need to support your lower back or head with a cushion please do so, .

In this practice we will be slowly moving our awareness through the body, noticing the immediate physical sensations that arise as we go. There is no right or wrong way for the body to feel in each moment, so whether pleasant, unpleasant or neutral sensations arise during the practice, the invitation here is just to allow the body to be as it is, and simply notice your experience as best you can, exploring the body with an open, curious and non-judgemental awareness.

Throughout the practice see if you can drop out of the level of concepts, ideas, and narratives about experience and into the more direct level of sensation: intimate, immediate and changing.

There will be times when the mind gets distracted, becoming lost in thought. This is perfectly natural. As soon as you notice this, without judgement, blame, or shame, we simply begin again, gently returning awareness to the immediate experience of the body with kindness.

We can begin by gently closing the eyes, or leaving them open with a soft, unfocused gaze if that feels more comfortable.

Take a few deeper breaths, settling into the body a little more with each outbreath. Now let the breath return to its natural rhythm, and get a broader sense of your experience in this moment. Noticing any sounds, noticing any lingering thoughts in the mind, getting a sense of any mood, or how we are holding ourselves in the body, with tightness, or with softness. Not trying to change how things are, but just acknowledging what is here in your experience. Take a few moments to give yourself some space around this, allowing your experience just to be as it is.

And now bringing awareness into the body, feeling the weight of the body on the ground or in the chair. Noticing any sensations of contact with the ground, maybe in the heels, the backs of the legs, the arms, shoulders and the back of the head. Feeling into this immediate connection with the earth beneath you. 

Noticing the body breathing. Notice where you feel the immediate sensations of breathing most directly - perhaps sensations of warmth and coolness at the nostrils as the air comes in and out, or of rising and falling at the belly. Let the body breathe all by itself, resting awareness on the simple sensations of breathing.

Now bringing your awareness to the toes of your left foot, starting with the left big toe. Feeling the immediate physical sensations in your left big toe. sensations of coolness, tingling, vibration, pulsing, warmth, or perhaps no sensations at all. We simply place attention on each part of the body, becoming open and curious as best you can, really feeling whatever sensations arise here. Now moving your awareness in the same way through the rest of the toes of the left foot.

Feeling into the top of the left foot. Noticing the sole of the left foot and the heel. Feeling into the left ankle and up the lower left leg.

Noticing the calf, and the shin. exploring any sensations in the lower left leg with curiosity, what does this part of the body actually feel like in this moment?

Moving up the left knee, the kneecap and back of the knee.

If the mind has become lost in thought, just noticing this, gently letting go of any thoughts, and returning awareness to the immediate experience of the body in this moment.

Guiding attention to the upper left leg, the front and back of the thigh, feeling any sensations up the left hip. Not trying to change, or alter experience in any way, simply holding whatever arises with a kind and open awareness.

Now taking a moment to feel into the entire left leg with awareness. Taking a few breaths as we rest awareness on the whole left leg.

Letting go of the left leg now, and bringing attention to the toes of the right foot, starting with the right big toe. Moving to the top of the right foot, around to the sole and heel of the foot, bringing a sense of curiosity to your experience of this part of the body, noticing any sensations in the right ankle and up the lower right leg.

Sensing into the right knee, the knee cap and back of the knee.

whenever you realise the mind has wandered, gently noticing without judgement where the mind has gone. before returning attention back to the body once again with kindness.

Moving up the right thigh to the right hip. Noticing any sensations of pressure in contact with the ground, or sensations of your clothes on your skin.

Sensing into the entire right leg now, taking a few breaths as you rest awareness on the right leg.

Now moving awareness up to the pelvis, and trunk of the body. Noticing any sensations of pressure, tightness, warmth, softness, pulsing, tingling. Becoming more curious about the body, what does it actually feel like in this moment?

Moving awareness to the lower back, noticing if we are holding any tension or tightness in this part of the body. Allowing whatever is here just to be as it is, as best you can. Letting go of any ideas, or narratives about our experience of the body and reconnecting with the felt sense of the body as it is in this moment.

Feeling up the lower back and around to the belly, moving attention through the abdomen.

If you experience any discomfort or unpleasant sensations at all in the body, notice if there is any reaction in the mind. Is there a sense of struggle or resistance present or do we fall into constructing a narrative around our experience?

The invitation in this practice is to turn towards any unpleasant sensations, exploring the actual physical sensations of pain or discomfort and noticing your experience as you do so.

What would it be like just to open to the experience of discomfort in the body, becoming curious about it, allowing it to be as it is, without resistance or struggle?

Get a sense of what is possible for you here, playing with your edge, staying with the actual immediate physical sensations that are unpleasant or painful with an open and gentle awareness, noticing and letting go of any stories in the mind as best you can.

And if things become too much, gently pulling back or moving on entirely. As always, listen to your own experience here.

Feeling into the upper torso now, the back and backs of the shoulders, as well as the chest area. Noticing any movement in this area of the body as the breath comes in and out.

If at any time during the practice you feel overwhelmed, or things become too much, ground yourself back in the immediate sensations of contact with the earth beneath you, or try resting awareness in the sensations in the palms of the hands. Stay here for as long as you need.

Getting a sense of the entire abdomen in awareness now, letting awareness settle on this part of the body, taking a few breaths here and noticing how that feels for you.

Letting go of the abdomen now and bringing attention to the sensations at the fingertips of both hands. Becoming curious about the tips of the fingers, perhaps there are sensations of tingling or pulsing, warmth or coolness, maybe there are quickly changing sensations or more slowly changing sensations, perhaps you can feel the air in contact with your skin. Scanning through the fingers into the palms of the hands, feeling into the sensations at the palms of the hands, the backs of the hands, the wrists and moving slowly up the forearms to the elbows.

If the mind has wandered, noticing this without judgement, letting go of any thoughts and stories in the mind, and returning once again to the simple intention to be aware of the body as it is moment after moment.

Bringing awareness now to the upper arms. Up the shoulders to the neck. Feeling the back of the neck, the front of the neck, the throat.

Noticing and letting go of any stories or commentary about your experience of the body, and reconnecting with the immediate felt sensations at the neck and throat.

Sensing now into the face, starting at the jaw and chin. Softening awareness around any tension here, letting it be. Feeling the chin, the lips, the cheeks, the nose, the eyes, the eyebrows, temples, the forehead. Becoming aware of the top of the head, the back of the head and the whole of the head, feeling into its weight, feeling into any sensations of heaviness, pressure, tingling.

Now just resting the mind right at the top of the crown of the head and expanding your awareness out from the top of the crown of the head to include the entire body resting in space. Gently holding the whole body from head to toe in this kind, open, spacious awareness. Letting the mind rest in this deep sense of embodiment. Noticing the flowing river of sensations that constitutes our body, allowing all of this just to be as it is.

For the final minute or so of this practice, let go of any and all sense of effort, not trying to fix the mind on anything in particular, not trying to hold anything together, or do anything at all. Just leaving everything as it is, allowing yourself just to be as you, awake and aware moment after moment.

And when you are ready, you can gently wiggle your fingers and toes, noticing what it is like to move the body once again in the space around you. And as you gently open the eyes, letting the colours and shapes of the room back into awareness, see if you can stay connected to this more embodied sense of presence as you proceed with your day.

This ends the body scan meditation. Thank you for your practice.
